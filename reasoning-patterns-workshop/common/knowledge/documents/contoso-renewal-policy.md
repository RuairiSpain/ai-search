# Contoso Commercial Policy CP-12: Renewals and Retention Offers
Version 3.2 — Effective 2026-01-01 — Owner: VP Commercial Operations

## 1. Scope
Applies to all renewal negotiations for enterprise and growth-tier subscription
contracts. Regulated-entity customers are additionally governed by CP-19.

## 2. Discount authority
2.1 Account managers may offer discounts up to the contract's discount cap.
2.2 Discounts above the cap require VP Commercial approval BEFORE the offer is
communicated. No system-generated offer may bypass this gate.
2.3 Discounts above 25% are prohibited regardless of approval level.

## 3. At-risk accounts
3.1 An account is "at risk" when 90-day usage trend is below −15% OR two or more
Severity-A tickets are open.
3.2 At-risk enterprise accounts require a retention plan reviewed by the regional
sales director before any renewal offer is sent.
3.3 Retention offers must reference open Severity-A incidents and include a
remediation commitment, not only a commercial concession.

## 4. Notice periods
4.1 Where a contract requires written renewal notice, the notice period in the
contract prevails over any standard timeline in this policy.
