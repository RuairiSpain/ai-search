# Contoso Policy CP-19: Regulated-Entity Addendum
Version 2.0 — Effective 2026-03-15 — Owner: Chief Compliance Officer

## 1. Applicability
Customers flagged as regulated entities (banking, insurance, healthcare payors).

## 2. Mandatory controls
2.1 Any commercial change (price, term, scope) requires compliance sign-off in
addition to the commercial approvals in CP-12.
2.2 EU-region regulated entities: data-residency confirmation must be attached
to any renewal above EUR 100,000 annual value.
2.3 AI-assisted recommendations for regulated entities must record the evidence
relied upon and the rules applied. Recommendations without a cited rule basis
are invalid.
