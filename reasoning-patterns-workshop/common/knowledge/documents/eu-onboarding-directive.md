# (Fictional) EU Financial Customer Onboarding Directive 2025/17 — Extracts
For workshop use only. Not real regulation.

Art. 4 — Risk-based due diligence. Institutions shall apply enhanced due
diligence where the assessed customer risk score meets or exceeds the
institution's enhanced-diligence threshold.

Art. 7 — Politically exposed persons. Business relationships with PEPs require
senior compliance approval and documented source-of-wealth checks.

Art. 9 — Verification precedence. No onboarding decision may be finalised
before identity verification completes; provisional drafts are permissible.

Art. 12 — Explainability. Automated or AI-assisted onboarding decisions shall
cite the specific control or rule motivating any restriction imposed.
