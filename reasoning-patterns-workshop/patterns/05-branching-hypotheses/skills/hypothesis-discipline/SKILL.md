# Branching discipline

- Every hypothesis names its eliminating evidence up front; unfalsifiable
  branches are narratives and get pruned first.
- Evidence discriminates BETWEEN branches: prefer the tool call whose result
  differs most across surviving hypotheses.
- Hypotheses may be partially true simultaneously; the verdict states the
  decomposition, not a forced winner.
- Delayed commitment is the feature, bounded budget is the guardrail: when the
  round cap hits, report survivors + what would resolve them, never a guess.
